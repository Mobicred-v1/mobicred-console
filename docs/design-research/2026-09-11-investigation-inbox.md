# Design research — investigation inbox — 2026-09-11

## Surface lock
- Route: `/` inbox
- Actor: internal operator
- In scope: empty, unauthorized, unavailable, live-list
- Out of scope: visual identity, Mifos rewrite, payment mutation

## Identity nucleus
- Mechanism: one case composed from several owning services
- Setting: desk investigation, not a marketing dashboard
- Product truth: disagreements between Core, gateway, and CI
- Constraint: never substitute demo data

## Convention and divergence
- Keep: familiar ops inbox (list, open, status)
- Reject: KPI wallpaper, silent demo fill, catch-all service proxy
- Own: owner chips that can disagree
- Reason: the job is reconciliation of systems, not a single score

## Beyond the object
- Object: a list of cases
- Verb: open a case and read owner slices
- Close: every owner reports live/stale/unavailable/unauthorized
- Quiet: empty tenant, no celebration
- Almost: unauthorized vs unavailable copy
- Refuse: confetti, fake counts, a combined “healthy” badge
